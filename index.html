What PharmaWise Does

Everything a Patient Needs to Know About Their Medications

From looking up a drug to scanning a prescription label, PharmaWise puts clear, trustworthy medication information in every patient's hands.

Core Feature

Drug Lookup & Plain-Language Education

Search any medication by name and get FDA-sourced drug information translated into plain language by AI — what it does, how to take it, side effects, and interactions, explained simply.

Learn more →

Prescription Label Scanning

Patients point their camera at a prescription label and PharmaWise reads it automatically — no typing needed. Drug name pre-fills instantly and a full explanation loads in seconds.

Learn more →

Pill Identifier

Unsure what a pill is? Patients photograph it and our AI analyses the imprint, shape, and colour to identify the medication and provide a full safety profile — no guessing.

Learn more →

Smart Medication Reminders

AI analyses a patient's full medication regimen alongside their wake and sleep schedule to recommend the optimal time for each drug — reducing missed doses and dangerous interactions.

Learn more →

Patient Medication Profiles

A secure, personal medication history — track active prescriptions, refill status, and interaction checks. Supports minor profiles and caretaker access for family health management.

Learn more →

Pharmacy Connect

PharmaWise links patients directly to their pharmacy — call, get directions, or request refills without leaving the app. Pharmacies get a branded patient engagement channel at no extra cost to patients.

Learn more →
