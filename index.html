The Challenge

Most patients leave the pharmacy without fully understanding their medications.

Medication non-adherence is one of the most preventable crises in healthcare — and it begins at the point of dispensing.

50% of patients do not take medications as prescribed1
$300B+ estimated annual cost of medication non-adherence in the US2
4.5B prescriptions filled annually — most without plain-language guidance3

1 World Health Organization. Adherence to Long-Term Therapies: Evidence for Action. 2003.

2 New England Healthcare Institute. Thinking Outside the Pillbox. 2009; Annals of Pharmacotherapy, 2012.

3 IQVIA National Prescription Audit, 2023.

Who We Are

A health technology company built from the pharmacy floor up.

Dibiax Health Technologies is a Maryland-based health technology company founded to close the gap between what is written on a prescription label and what patients actually understand about their medications.

Our flagship product, PharmaWise, is a HIPAA-compliant, AI-powered platform that connects pharmacy patients to clear, actionable medication information — directly through their pharmacy's existing workflow.

We believe that a well-informed patient is a safer patient. Every feature we build is guided by that principle.

Our Founder

Emeka Emenanjo

Founder & CEO, Dibiax Health Technologies · Licensed Pharmacist

Emeka Emenanjo is a licensed pharmacist with direct patient-facing experience in medication counseling and safety. He founded Dibiax Health Technologies after recognising a consistent gap between what is written on prescription labels and what patients actually understand when they leave the pharmacy.

Rather than waiting for someone else to solve it, Emeka combined his clinical expertise with full-stack software development to build PharmaWise — a platform designed from the ground up around the real-world needs of pharmacy patients.

"Every patient deserves to understand the medication they are putting in their body. PharmaWise exists to make that a reality."
